## Current video output

The actual model is MiniMax-H3. Current output is fixed at 10 seconds and 768P. Identity and motion can vary; review the result before sharing.

## Paid generation

Sign-in is required. Each 10-second, 768P video costs 50 purchased credits, reserved before generation starts. There is no free generation allowance and no daily generation limit. Failed or canceled generation automatically restores the reserved credits. Storage retries do not charge additional credits.

## Purchased credits

Buy credit packs on the pricing page. Waffo Pancake processes payment, and credits are granted after confirmation. Saved videos are archived to private R2 storage and are available through your account. See the [privacy policy](/privacy-policy) and [terms](/terms-of-service).
