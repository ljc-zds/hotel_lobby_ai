The actual model is MiniMax-H3. Current output is fixed at 10 seconds and 768P. Identity and motion can vary; review the result before sharing.

## Limited free launch

Sign-in is required. Two submission attempts per account per UTC day and ten attempts across the service per UTC day are available. Failed attempts count. Video generation does not charge money or use displayed demo credits.

## Credit-pack demonstrations

Pricing buttons add local demo credits without payment. They do not grant additional real generation quota. Browser history stores result links; account-linked task metadata is stored on the server. Input photos are not saved to these records. Download completed videos promptly because provider result links expire. See the [privacy policy](/privacy-policy) and [terms](/terms-of-service).
