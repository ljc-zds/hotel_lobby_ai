## Three output options

The interface offers 480p, 720p and 1080p, showing costs of 20, 45 and 80 credits. These choices demonstrate the workflow. The template does not generate new AI videos.

## Choosing a resolution

Lower resolutions can help review composition, while higher resolutions can show more detail. Real quality also depends on input photos and the generation service that is later connected.

## Demo purchases

Pricing buttons add demo credits to the current browser. No payment, billing record or subscription is created.

## Where records are stored

Demo credits and creation records use browser localStorage. They do not sync between devices and are removed when site data is cleared. Uploaded photos are not written to these records.

## Connecting a real service

A production service needs server-side credit accounting, payment verification and generation records. Do not treat browser demo data as authoritative billing. Explore [pricing](/pricing) or [my videos](/orders) to try the interface.
