## A clear video format

Two subjects, a hanging microphone in the center and a fixed landscape camera. The subjects perform in turn to the music, emphasizing their shared interaction.

## Start in three steps

1. Choose a scene in the [template library](/#templates).
2. Add two clear, authorized photos in the [generator](/#video-generator).
3. Sign in, confirm your rights to the photos and submit a 15-second, 768P generation request.

## What a template provides

Templates provide the setting, visual atmosphere and a performance reference. With MiniMax-H3, identity, motion and lip sync may still vary. Review the entire output before sharing.

## Real video generation

Sign-in is required. Each 15-second, 768P video costs 50 purchased credits, reserved before generation starts. There is no free generation allowance and no daily generation limit. Failed or canceled generation automatically restores the reserved credits. Storage retries do not charge additional credits.
