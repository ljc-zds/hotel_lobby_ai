## Reference Photos and MiniMax

Photos remain in browser memory during preview. When you confirm authorization and submit a generation request, the browser resizes the two photos and sends them through our authenticated server to MiniMax's Video V2 API using MiniMax-H3. Please do not upload sensitive or unauthorized images. Our application does not save the original input photos to its database or object storage. MiniMax processes the submitted references under its own privacy policy and service terms; we do not promise that its processing, retention or training practices match ours.

We store account-linked task identifiers, scene, model, status, timestamps, preset prompt and result links to provide generation status and resolve support requests. Completed videos are copied from the provider to private Cloudflare R2 storage. Playback links require sign-in and are checked against the owning account. Browser storage may retain the active task identifier to resume status checks; your video library is linked to your account. Contact us to request deletion of account-linked task records; legal and abuse-prevention obligations may require retaining limited records.

## Visitor Statistics

We use Google Analytics (measurement ID G-7F8T3C2J0R) and a separate first-party visitor counter. The counter creates a random browser identifier in local storage for up to 30 days and stores a keyed hash, page-view event identifiers, public page paths and daily totals for up to 90 days. This statistics store does not contain photos, email addresses, IP addresses or query-string contents. Only authorized administrators can access its reports. Counts begin when this counter is deployed; they are not historical Google Analytics data.

The first-party counter skips account and admin pages and browsers that enable Do Not Track. Blocking storage prevents this counter from recording a visit; clearing site data resets the identifier. Google Analytics has separate processing and controls, including the [Google Analytics opt-out add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).
