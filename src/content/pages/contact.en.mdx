## Report content violations

Email [2670855280@qq.com](mailto:2670855280@qq.com?subject=HotelLobby%20Content%20Report) with the subject **HotelLobby Content Report**. Include the content URL or creation identifier, the suspected violation, a brief explanation and relevant timestamps. Redact unrelated personal information. Do not attach illegal material or private photos; report child-safety concerns using a URL and description.

The platform operator reviews reports manually and may request additional information. Confirmed violations may result in removal or restriction of hosted content, a warning, access restrictions or account suspension. Photos held only in browser memory before submission cannot be remotely reviewed or deleted. Read our [content standards and report handling process](/terms-of-service) for details and appeal instructions.
