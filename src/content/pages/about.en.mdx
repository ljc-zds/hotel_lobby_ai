## Two photos, one shared stage

HotelLobby.ai helps friends, couples, pet owners and creative teams create a short video from two authorized reference photos and a preset performance scene.

## Actual video model

**Provider: MiniMax. Model: MiniMax-H3, through the Video V2 API.** Generation creates a 10-second, 768P video. Sign-in is required. Each account can submit two attempts per UTC day, and the service accepts ten attempts per UTC day in total. Failed attempts count. This limited launch is free; displayed credit packs and checkout remain demonstrations.

Reference photos are resized in the browser and submitted only when you request generation. Our application does not persist input photos. We store account-linked task metadata and temporary result links. Read our [privacy policy](/privacy-policy) for details. Model outputs can contain inaccuracies and download links expire.
