## A clear video format

Two subjects, a hanging microphone in the center and a fixed landscape camera. The subjects perform in turn to the music, emphasizing their shared interaction.

## Start in three steps

1. Choose a scene in the [template library](/#templates).
2. Add two clear, authorized photos in the [generator](/#video-generator).
3. Choose 480p, 720p or 1080p and confirm your rights to the photos.

## What a template provides

Templates provide the setting, visual atmosphere and a performance reference. After a real generation provider is connected, identity, motion and lip sync may still vary. Review the entire output before sharing.

## What this demo does

Browse templates, preview the first sample video, add local photos and explore the workflow. It does not generate a new video or charge you.
