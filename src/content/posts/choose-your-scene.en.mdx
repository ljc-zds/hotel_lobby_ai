## Start with the atmosphere

An orange studio puts the subjects and microphone first. Hotel lounges and marble reception areas add more context and suit formal clothing.

## Pet scenes

The animal category includes cats, foxes, red pandas and corgis. Look at subject placement and the camera position before deciding whether your photos fit the scene.

## Preview and select

Click an image to open a preview, then choose “Create this style” to use the scene in the generator. Only the first scene has a sample video in this template; other scenes display posters. A poster helps you choose a composition and is not a promise of output quality.

## Keep the intent consistent

Everyday clothing can fit a street studio, while formal clothing can fit a lounge. Avoid contradictory scene or movement instructions.

## Start creating

Browse the [template library](/#templates), narrow the choices with category buttons and add two authorized photos.
